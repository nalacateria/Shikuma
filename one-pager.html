Principals

Juan Benet is a frontier technology founder and investor. He founded Protocol Labs in 2014 (Y Combinator, Summer 2014)1, an R&D lab for computer networks, and created IPFS, the peer-to-peer content-addressed protocol, and Filecoin, a decentralized storage network. He was named to Fortune’s 40 Under 40 in 20182 and is an active angel investor in frontier technology.

Molly Mackinlay is Head of R&D at Protocol Labs and CEO of FilOz, where she leads development of Filecoin Onchain Cloud.

Philanthropy

Shikuma’s giving focuses on accelerating scientific progress: fast, flexible funding for researchers, open infrastructure for science, and the institutions that make new fields possible.

  • Impetus Grants — Juan Benet and Molly Mackinlay are both publicly listed patrons3 of Impetus Grants, which gives scientists fast grants for longevity research that would otherwise go unfunded. The program reports $34M deployed across 146 projects, decisions within three weeks, and 250+ resulting papers and preprints.3
  • Open science — support for open, verifiable research infrastructure and the public goods that new scientific fields depend on.
  • Additional causes across science and technology for the public good.
Investments

Shikuma has made 75+ investments in frontier technology companies. It invests early, alongside founders working on hard technical problems, and acts as a long-term partner as research becomes product and companies scale.
